THE DRIVING FORCE

Co-Founder Farhan — Introducing RUHI

Every brand begins with an idea, but behind every meaningful idea is someone who chooses to believe in it, build it, and keep moving forward when the journey gets difficult.

Farhan is one of the driving forces behind RUHI Fragrances — a young fragrance brand built with a simple yet powerful vision: to create perfumes that are more than just a scent, but a feeling, a memory, and a reflection of the person wearing them.

From the early stages of planning and developing the brand to building its identity, product collection, and customer experience, Farhan has been deeply involved in the journey of RUHI. His focus has always been on creating something that feels premium, modern, meaningful, and accessible.

"RUHI represents the belief that fragrance has the power to become a part of your identity. Every bottle is created with the intention of making people feel confident, unique, and connected to their own personality."

As Co-Founder, Farhan brings together vision, creativity, determination, and a passion for fragrances. His goal is not simply to launch another perfume brand, but to build RUHI into a name that people remember, trust, and emotionally connect with.

The journey of RUHI is only beginning. Starting with the exclusive 8ML Tester Collection featuring the Top 10 Fragrances, the brand is taking its first step toward a much bigger vision.

There will be challenges, new ideas, improvements, and countless milestones ahead — but the purpose remains the same:

To create fragrances that tell your story.

RUHI Fragrances — The Fragrance of Soul.

And behind this journey stands Farhan, Co-Founder of RUHI, turning an idea into a brand, one fragrance at a time.
